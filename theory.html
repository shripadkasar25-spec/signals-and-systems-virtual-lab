Seismic Building Vibration Signal Analysis

Time-Domain and Frequency-Domain Signal Processing

1. Fundamental Principles

A seismic building vibration signal represents the vibration of a building due to earthquake or ground motion. Signal processing helps analyze its amplitude, intensity, frequency, noise, natural frequency, and energy in both time and frequency domains.

2. Mathematical Formulation

For a sampled signal x[n]:

Peak amplitude:

Apeak = max |x[n]|

RMS value:

XRMS = √[(1/N) ∑n=1Nx2[n]]

Energy:

E = ∑n=1N|x[n]|2

Natural frequency:

fn = (1/2π)√(k/m)

FFT: Converts the signal from the time domain to the frequency domain to identify its dominant frequency.

3. Application

The analysis is used to monitor building vibrations, detect abnormal vibrations, identify natural frequencies, remove noise, and study structural response during earthquakes.

4. Key Insights

  • Peak and RMS indicate vibration intensity.
  • FFT identifies dominant frequency components.
  • Filtering reduces unwanted noise.
  • Natural frequency depends mainly on building mass and stiffness.
  • Energy indicates the overall strength of the vibration signal.
