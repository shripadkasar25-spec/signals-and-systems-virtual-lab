Experiment Aim

To analyze a seismic building vibration signal using time-domain and frequency-domain signal processing techniques, and to determine its important characteristics such as amplitude, RMS value, statistical parameters, dominant frequency, natural frequency, filtering response, and signal energy.

Objectives:

  • To analyze the signal in the time domain.
  • To calculate RMS, statistical parameters, and energy.
  • To obtain the frequency spectrum and dominant frequency using FFT.
  • To study filtering and determine the building's natural frequency.
